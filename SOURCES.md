# Sources and attribution

Replace this page before publishing a real book. Record the primary text,
license, and any third-party figures or media here so readers can trace
provenance.

## Primary text

Describe the source work (author, title, edition, URL) and its license.
Example:

> This MyST edition adapts *[Title]* by [Author], available at
> [URL] under [license].

## Figures and external resources

Figures derive from the openly licensed source edition unless a caption states
otherwise. New third-party material must carry creator, source, and license
information in its caption or in this ledger.
